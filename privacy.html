Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 18, 2026

This policy explains what information is handled when you play Place It Organize Stuff ("the game") on Android or iOS, published by Unik Studio ("we").

Summary

  • We do not ask you to create an account and we do not run our own servers for this game.
  • Your progress is saved on your device. When you are signed in to Google Play Games (Android) or iCloud (iOS), a backup copy is kept in that account of yours; we never receive it, and you can turn it off in Settings.
  • The game shows ads through Google AdMob. Google collects some device data to deliver and measure ads.
  • Optional purchases are processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We never see your card details.
  • Leaderboards are optional: your scores are sent to Google Play Games (Android) or Apple Game Center (iOS) only after you tap "Join the leaderboards".
  • Reminder notifications are optional and created on your device only. No notification data leaves your device.

1. Data stored on your device

The game stores the following in the app's local storage on your device. It is not sent to us.

  • Game progress: levels cleared, stars, coins, hearts, pets, items and decorations you own, daily rewards and missions.
  • Settings: language, sound, play mode.
  • Records of completed purchases and daily ad-reward counts, so rewards are granted once.
  • Play statistics for game balancing (for example which level was started, won or lost, and how long it took), tagged with a random 8-character code created on your device. These statistics stay on the device. They leave it only if you choose to export the file and share it yourself.

On Android, your progress may be included in the device backup of your Google account if you have backup turned on. That backup is controlled by you and Google.

Deleting the app or clearing its data erases this information. It cannot be recovered by us.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows rewarded ads that you choose to watch, and may show ads between levels. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To serve, limit and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google's SDK may collect and process:

Advertising ID (Android) or IDFA (iOS, only if you allow tracking)Ad delivery, personalised ads where permitted, frequency capping, measurement
IP address, from which an approximate location (country, region, city) is derivedAd selection, fraud prevention, legal compliance

Diagnostics (crash and performance data of the ads SDK)Keeping the SDK working

Google acts as an independent controller for this data. See Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Your choices. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are used. On iOS, the system asks whether you allow tracking; if you decline, the game works the same and ads are not personalised with your IDFA. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings (Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads; iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking).

3. Purchases

You can buy coin packs, a starter pack and "Remove ads". Payment is handled entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store under their own terms and privacy policies. We do not receive your name, payment card or billing address. The game receives a purchase confirmation (product identifier and purchase token or transaction ID) from the store and checks it on your device to grant what you bought.

3a. Leaderboards (optional)

The game has leaderboards for comparing progress with other players. By default the game sends nothing. Only after you tap "Join the leaderboards" in the "Your records" panel does the game send four numbers to the platform's games service: levels cleared, total stars, new levels cleared this week, and levels completed with 3 stars on the first clear this week.

On Android that service is Google Play Games; on iOS it is Apple Game Center. Your scores appear with the nickname and avatar you set in Play Games or Game Center, according to your visibility settings there. Google or Apple store these scores under their own policies (Google, Apple). We do not receive your name, email or account ID, and we keep no copy of your scores on any server.

You can tap "Stop taking part" at any time and the game stops sending scores. To delete scores already sent, use the Google Play Games settings (delete the game's Play Games data) or Game Center. Leaderboard ranks are never exchanged for coins or rewards in the game.

3b. Notifications (optional)

If you turn them on, the game can show a few local reminders: when your hearts are full again, when garden or farm items are ready, when the daily gift and new quests are available, and a gentle reminder after 1 and 3 days without playing. At most 2 a day, never between 9 PM and 8 AM.

These are local notifications: the game calculates the times from the progress saved on your device and asks your phone's own notification system (Android AlarmManager / iOS UserNotifications) to show them. Nothing is sent to us or to any server, there is no push service, no notification token is created, and nothing about notifications is shared with advertisers.

The game asks for notification permission only after explaining it in the game, never when you first open it. You can turn all notifications, or each kind, off in the game's Settings → Notifications, or in your device settings.

3d. Progress backup (optional)

So that you do not lose your progress when you change or reset your phone, the game can keep one backup copy of your game progress (levels cleared, stars, coins, pets, garden, farm, items you own and the list of purchases already delivered) in your own account: Google Play Games Saved Games on Android, or your iCloud on iOS. The backup is used only to restore your progress.

On Android this happens only if you are signed in to Google Play Games; on iOS only if your device is signed in to iCloud. The copy is stored by Google or Apple under their own policies (Google, Apple). We do not receive this data and we do not run a server for it. The game never replaces the progress on your device with a backup without asking you first.

You can turn automatic backup off in the game's Settings → Back up progress. To delete the backup, remove the game's saved data in the Google Play Games app (Settings → Delete Play Games account & data → this game) or in iCloud settings (Manage Storage → this game).

3c. App updates

The game is updated only through Google Play or the Apple App Store; it never downloads code from anywhere else. On Android, when the game starts it asks Google Play (In-App Updates) whether a newer version exists; Google Play then shows its own update screen and handles the download under Google's terms. On iOS, the game asks Apple's public App Store lookup service whether a newer version exists. This request contains only the game's bundle identifier and your device's store region (Apple sees your IP address, as with any connection); no personal data, identifier or game progress is sent. If an update exists, tapping "Update" opens the game's App Store page.

4. What we do not do

  • No account, no contact list, no photos, no microphone, no precise location.
  • We do not sell personal data.
  • The game does not currently use Firebase or any other analytics service that sends data to us. If that changes, this policy will be updated before the change takes effect.

5. Children

The game is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we will help.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under GDPR, UK GDPR or CCPA/CPRA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal data. Because we do not hold your game data on our servers, most requests are fulfilled by deleting the app's data on your device. For data held by Google (including Play Games leaderboard scores and the optional progress backup) or by Apple (Game Center, iCloud backup), use Google's or Apple's tools. You can also contact us and we will assist.

7. Security and retention

Game data stays on your device and is kept until you delete the app or its data. Data collected by Google is retained under Google's policies.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version. Significant changes will be announced in the game or on the store page.
